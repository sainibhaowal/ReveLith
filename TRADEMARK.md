---

## 1. Permitted Uses (No Permission Needed)

You may use the ReveLith name and unmodified logos without prior written consent in the following situations:

- **Factual References**: Truthfully stating that your software runs on, integrates with, or is compatible with ReveLith (e.g., *"Extension for ReveLith"*, *"Compatible with ReveLith"*).
- **Linking & Distribution**: Linking directly to official ReveLith repositories or downloads ([github.com/sainibhaowal/ReveLith](https://github.com/sainibhaowal/ReveLith)).
- **Community Discussion**: Referencing ReveLith in tutorials, articles, presentations, or educational content.

---

## 2. Prohibited Uses (Without Written Permission)

To prevent confusion among users and protect project integrity, the following are strictly prohibited:

- **Confusingly Similar Product Names**: You may not name forks, spin-offs, or commercial distributions *"ReveLith Official"*, *"ReveLith Pro"*, *"ReveLith Suite"*, or any name likely to mislead users into believing it is produced or endorsed by ReveLith.
- **Modified Logos**: You may not distort, recolor, alter, or incorporate the ReveLith logo into a third-party logo or company mark.
- **Domain Names & Social Handles**: Registering domain names (e.g., `revelith-download.com`) or official-sounding social media handles containing "ReveLith".
- **Misleading Endorsement**: Using the name or logo in any way that implies sponsorship, official partnership, or certification without explicit agreement.

---

## 3. Rules for Forks and Modified Versions

If you distribute a modified build or fork of the source code:
- You must remove the official ReveLith branding, app icons, and logos from the binary distribution.
- You may include a factual statement such as: *"Based on the ReveLith open-source project"*.

---

## 4. Questions & Permission Inquiries

If you would like to request permission for a use case not covered by this document, please contact the project maintainers via GitHub issues or repository contact channels.
